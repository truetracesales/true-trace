What we do

Two kinds of check, for the two decisions that carry the most risk.

Candidate screening

Before you hire, we check what the candidate has told you against what the public record shows.

  • Identity and public profile consistency
  • Qualifications and professional registrations
  • Directorships and business interests
  • Public court and media records

Company due diligence

Before you sign, we look at the company you plan to work with and the people behind it.

  • Registration, ownership and directors
  • Related companies and other interests
  • Litigation and regulatory notices
  • Reputation in public sources

How we work

From your question to a report you can act on.

  1. STEP 1

    Brief

    You tell us who or what to check, and what decision depends on it. We confirm scope and consent.

  2. STEP 2

    Collect

    We search public sources only. Every search and result is logged as we go.

  3. STEP 3

    Verify

    Findings are cross-checked against a second source before they reach the report.

  4. STEP 4

    Report

    You receive clear, sourced facts. No rumour, no opinion presented as evidence.

Our standards

Careful with information, because people's livelihoods depend on it.

Public information only

We do not break into accounts, buy leaked data or use pretexting. If it isn't publicly available, it isn't in our report.

Handled lawfully

Checks on individuals are done with a lawful basis, and personal information is kept only as long as the work needs it.

Reported as sourced facts

Each statement points to where we found it and when, so your team can check our work and stand behind its decision.

What you get

A written report that separates what was confirmed, what could not be found and what does not match, with sources for each.

What we do not do

We do not recommend whether to hire or contract. That decision stays with you. We give you the facts to make it well.
